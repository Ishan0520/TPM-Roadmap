## ⚙️ Phase 1 — Vehicle Engineering Fundamentals

> Understand the five core subsystems of a vehicle from first principles. Teardown before theory.

**Timeline:** Months 1–3

<details>
<summary><strong>📌 Core Concepts to Master (14 Domains)</strong></summary>

| Domain | Key Topics |
|--------|-----------|
| **Powertrain — ICE** | Otto/Diesel cycles, torque/power curves, transmissions (MT/AT/CVT/DCT), turbocharging, exhaust aftertreatment |
| **Powertrain — EV** | PMSM vs. induction motors, inverters (IGBT/SiC), reduction gearboxes, regenerative braking |
| **Battery Chemistry** | NMC, LFP, LMFP, NCA, solid-state; C-rate, SOC, SOH, depth of discharge, calendar vs. cycle aging |
| **Chassis & Suspension** | MacPherson strut, double-wishbone, multi-link; camber/caster/toe; spring rates, damper tuning |
| **Braking Systems** | ABS, EBD, ESC, brake-by-wire, regenerative blending, brake fade physics, thermal limits |
| **Steering Systems** | Electric power steering (EPS), steer-by-wire, Ackermann geometry, steering ratio, road feel feedback |
| **Body & Safety Structures** | Crumple zones, HSLA steel, aluminum, CFRP, NCAP test modes (frontal, side, pole, pedestrian) |
| **NVH (Noise/Vibration/Harshness)** | Source-path-receiver model, modal analysis, damping materials, acoustic targets, psychoacoustics |
| **E/E Architecture** | ECU topology, CAN / LIN / FlexRay / Automotive Ethernet, harness design, grounding strategies |
| **Thermal Management** | Coolant loops, heat pumps, battery thermal management, HVAC refrigerant cycles (vapor compression) |
| **Aerodynamics** | Drag coefficient (Cd), lift/downforce, underbody flow management, active aero systems |
| **Materials Science** | Yield strength, fatigue life, galvanic corrosion, joining methods (welding, riveting, adhesive) |
| **Manufacturing Processes** | Stamping, die casting, injection molding, welding types, gigacasting, roll forming |
| **Vehicle Dynamics** | Understeer/oversteer, slip angle, lateral g-force, yaw moment diagram, weight transfer |

</details>

## 📋 Phase 3 — Platform & Program Management

> How vehicles are planned, engineered, and launched as multi-year, multi-billion dollar programs across hundreds of organizations.

| **APQP** | Advanced Product Quality Planning — 5 phases from program launch to production validation |

## 💡 Phase 4 — Technology Frontier

> The three forces transforming every OEM: **Electrification**, **Software-Defined Vehicles**, and **Autonomous Driving / ADAS**.

**Timeline:** Year 1–2

<details>
<summary><strong>📌 Core Concepts to Master</strong></summary>

### ⚡ Electrification

| Concept | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| **Battery Cell Chemistry** | NMC 811, LFP, LMFP, NCA, solid-state; energy density vs. cost vs. safety trade-offs |
| **Pack Architecture** | CTM → CTP → CTB progression; structural integration; thermal interface materials |
| **BMS Architecture** | Passive/active cell balancing, SOC/SOH estimation, thermal control loops, fault management |

| **OTA Update Architecture** | Full-stack vs. firmware OTA; rollback logic; regulatory compliance; campaign management |
| **High-Performance Compute (HPC)** | NVIDIA DRIVE, Qualcomm Snapdragon Ride, Mobileye EyeQ, custom SoCs |

## 🛡️ Phase 5 — Regulatory, Safety & Sustainability

> Compliance is the floor. Leaders who anticipate the next regulatory cycle gain 3–5 year advantages over competitors.

**Timeline:** Year 2

<details>
<summary><strong>📌 Core Concepts to Master</strong></summary>

| Standard / Concept | Scope | Key Elements |
|-------------------|-------|--------------|
| **ISO 26262** | Functional safety — road vehicles | ASIL A/B/C/D, hazard analysis, safety goals, FTTI, SIL decomposition |
| **ISO 21448 (SOTIF)** | Safety of intended functionality — ADAS | Known/unknown unsafe scenarios, validation sufficiency argument |
| **IEC 61508** | Parent functional safety standard | SIL 1–4 framework that ISO 26262 derives from |
| **UNECE WP.29 R155** | Cybersecurity management system | CSMS type approval mandatory in EU, Korea, Japan |
| **UNECE WP.29 R156** | Software update management | SUMS type approval — OTA legal framework |
| **ISO/SAE 21434** | Automotive cybersecurity | TARA, CAL A/B/C/D, penetration testing methodology |
| **SAE J3016** | Driving automation taxonomy | Levels 0–5 — know the precise definitions, not the popular misunderstandings |
| **FMVSS** | US federal safety standards | NHTSA enforcement, homologation requirements for US market |
| **ECE Regulations** | European safety & emissions | Euro NCAP, WLTP, RDE testing, type approval process |
| **CAFE / EPA Standards** | US fuel economy & emissions | Fleet-average compliance; ZEV credits; CARB waiver states |
| **EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542** | Battery sustainability | Battery passport, carbon footprint, recycled content mandates, due diligence |
| **CENELEC EN 50126/50128/50129** | Rail functional safety (RAMS) | SIL 1–4 for safety-critical rail signaling systems |
| **ELV Directive** | End-of-life vehicles | Recyclability targets, restricted materials (lead/mercury/cadmium), design for disassembly |

</details>
